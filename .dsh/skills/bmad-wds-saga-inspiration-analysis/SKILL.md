# Inspiration Analysis Workshop (Product Brief)

**Original source:** `_bmad/wds/data/agent-guides/saga/inspiration-analysis.md`

**When to load:** After Visual Direction, as final Product Brief companion document

## Why This Matters

Without documented visual/UX preferences from real examples, Dream Up agents must guess what the client likes.

**The power:** When a client says "I like that layout" pointing at a real site, you have a concrete reference — not abstract words.

## What We Need to Know

1. **Documented reactions to real sites** — specific elements liked/disliked with WHY
2. **Visual style preferences** — from concrete examples
3. **Layout and structure patterns** — what arrangements appeal
4. **UX patterns** — what interaction patterns they prefer
5. **Anti-patterns** — what they've explicitly rejected
6. **Synthesized design principles** — strategic takeaways

**Quality bar:**
- At least 2 sites analyzed
- For each site: specific elements with client's reaction
- Synthesized principles clear enough for a Dream Up agent to self-review against

## The Process

### Getting URLs
Ask the client for 2-4 sites they find inspiring. If hesitant: even one site with one thing they like is valuable. If no references: offer to find 2-3 examples in their industry.

### Analyzing Each Site
1. **Load the site** — use browser/WebFetch tools
2. **Ask open first** — "What drew you to this site?" Let the client lead.
3. **Get specific** — ask about what's actually there: layout, navigation, content display, visual style, specific elements, performance
4. **Capture nuance** — listen for: Approval ("like that"), Rejection ("don't like that"), Conditional ("like but..."). The WHY behind each reaction is more valuable than the reaction itself.
5. **Extract principles** — as patterns emerge across sites, identify strategic takeaways.

### Synthesizing
After all sites, organize findings into design principles by category:
- Layout & Structure
- Visual Style & Color
- Typography & Text
- Navigation & Interaction
- Imagery & Photography
- Performance & Load

## Document Template

```markdown
## Inspiration Analysis

### Sites Analyzed
1. [URL] — [Overall impression]
2. [URL] — [Overall impression]

### Design Principles Synthesized
**Layout & Structure:**
- [Principle derived from client reactions]
  - Why: [rationale]
  - Inspired by: [site reference]

**Visual Style:**
- [Principle]
  - Why: [rationale]

### Anti-Patterns (Avoid)
- [Pattern to avoid] — "Don't like [specific element] on [site]"
```

## Success Criteria
- Client has reacted to specific elements from real examples
- Preferences documented with concrete references (not abstract words)
- Design principles clear and actionable
- Anti-patterns explicitly documented
- Dream Up agents can reference documented preferences and self-review against them
