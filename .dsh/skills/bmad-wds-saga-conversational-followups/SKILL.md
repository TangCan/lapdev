# Conversational Follow-Up Patterns

**Original source:** `_bmad/wds/data/agent-guides/saga/conversational-followups.md`

**When to load:** During any Product Brief step where you need to explore user thinking through follow-up questions

**Companion to:** `discovery-conversation.md` (general principles)

## Core Philosophy

**Don't ask users to produce outputs (vision statements, positioning statements, frameworks). Instead:**
1. Have exploratory conversations where they dump their ideas
2. Ask intelligent follow-ups based on what emerges
3. YOU synthesize the substance into formal artifacts

**Spirit:** "Just dump your ideas, I'll help structure your thinking"

## Signal-Based Follow-Up Patterns

### When They Mention USERS or CUSTOMERS
**Signals:** Names a specific type of person, describes a frustration or need, "for people who..."
**Follow-ups:** "Tell me more about them", "What frustrates them today?", "What would their life look like if this existed?", "How do they currently solve this problem?", "Walk me through a typical day for them"

### When They Mention BUSINESS VALUE
**Signals:** Revenue, growth, efficiency, ROI, cost savings, market share
**Follow-ups:** "How do you see that translating to revenue?", "What would success look like in 6 months?", "What's the business impact if you DON'T do this?", "What's currently costing you time/money?"

### When They Mention a SPECIFIC FEATURE or CONCEPT
**Signals:** "We want to have X", describes specific functionality
**Follow-ups:** "What problem does that solve?", "Why is that important to you?", "Can you walk me through how that would work?", "Is there a simpler way?", "What happens if we don't have that?"

### When They Mention COMPETITORS or MARKET
**Signals:** Names a competitor, "Like X but...", market positioning
**Follow-ups:** "What do they do well?", "Where do they fall short?", "What would make yours different?", "What frustrates users about their solution?"

### When They Express EMOTIONS
**Signals:** "I'm excited/worried/frustrated...", tone indicators
**Follow-ups:** "What makes you [emotion] about that?", "Tell me more about why that matters", "What would it feel like if this worked perfectly?"

### When They Use VAGUE LANGUAGE
**Signals:** "make it user-friendly", "improve the experience", "modern design"
**Follow-ups:** "Can you give me a specific example?", "What does 'user-friendly' look like for your users?", "Walk me through a scenario where that matters"

## Key Principles

- **Follow the signal, not the step** — Let what emerges guide your next question
- **One question at a time** — Wait for response before moving on
- **Reflect back** — "So what I'm hearing is..." before asking follow-up
- **Synthesize at the end** — Pull everything into structured artifact

## Reflection Checkpoint

Before proceeding, pause and synthesize:
"Here's what I'm hearing across our conversation: [summarize key themes and signals]. Does that capture it?"
