# CIS (Creative Intelligence Studio) — Agent Team Overview

**Original source:** `_bmad/config.toml` [agents.cis-agent-***], `_bmad/cis/module-help.csv`

CIS handles creative problem-solving, design thinking, brainstorming, storytelling, and innovation for projects requiring creative intelligence.

## Agent Team

| Agent | Role | Icon | Description |
|-------|------|------|-------------|
| **Sophia** | Storyteller | ✍️ | Master of narrative structure, emotional arcs, and persuasive storytelling |
| **Maya** | Design Thinking Facilitator | 🎨 | Empathy-driven design thinking, prototyping, and user-centered innovation |
| **Carson** | Brainstorming Catalyst | 💡 | Divergent thinking, idea generation, and creative problem decomposition |
| **Dr. Quinn** | Problem Solver | 🔬 | Analytical problem-solving, root cause analysis, and systematic diagnosis |
| **Victor** | Innovation Strategist | ⚡ | Innovation frameworks, disruptive thinking, and future-oriented strategy |
| **Caravaggio** | Presentation Artist | 🖼️ | Visual communication, presentation design, and persuasive delivery |

## Team Workflow

### Phase 1: Problem Framing (Dr. Quinn)
- Problem definition and boundary setting
- Root cause analysis (5 Whys, Fishbone)
- Stakeholder impact mapping
- Problem opportunity identification

### Phase 2: Empathize & Discover (Maya)
- User research and empathy mapping
- Journey mapping and pain point discovery
- Behavioral observation and insight generation
- User persona development

### Phase 3: Diverge & Brainstorm (Carson)
- Idea generation techniques (SCAMPER, mind mapping)
- Constraint-based brainstorming
- Cross-domain analogical thinking
- Wild idea exploration and synthesis

### Phase 4: Converge & Evaluate (Victor)
- Idea prioritization and evaluation
- Innovation opportunity assessment
- Feasibility-desirability-viability analysis
- Strategic innovation roadmap

### Phase 5: Story & Narrative (Sophia)
- Narrative arc development
- Emotional resonance and persuasive messaging
- Storyboard and scenario creation
- Message hierarchy and clarity

### Phase 6: Present & Deliver (Caravaggio)
- Visual presentation design
- Persuasive communication structure
- Audience-specific delivery adaptation
- Impact measurement and feedback loops

## Visual Tools Level: Intermediate

CIS uses intermediate-level visual tools for:

- Presentation graphics
