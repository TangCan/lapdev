# GDS Market Research — Step-by-Step Workflow

**Original source:** `_bmad/gds/workflows/1-preproduction/research/market-steps/`

A 6-step game market research workflow. Each step requires web search verification and user confirmation before proceeding.

## Mandatory Rules

- NEVER generate content without web search verification
- ALWAYS read the complete step file before taking any action
- Present [C] continue option after content generation
- Update frontmatter `stepsCompleted` before loading next step
- FORBIDDEN to load next step until C is selected

## Step 1: Market Research Scope Confirmation

**Role:** Game Market Research Planner
**Focus:** Scope confirmation — understand the research topic, goals, and approach

Confirm scope covering:
- Target player segments and demographics
- Market size and growth trends
- Genre landscape and market positioning
- Distribution platforms and channels
- Revenue models and monetization patterns

## Step 2: Customer Behavior Analysis

**Role:** Player Behavior Analyst
**Focus:** Understanding how players behave, what motivates them, and how they engage

**Research areas:**
- Player engagement patterns and session behavior
- Platform preferences and usage habits
- Social and community behavior patterns
- Discovery and acquisition channels
- Retention and lifecycle behavior

## Step 3: Customer Pain Points

**Role:** Player Needs Analyst
**Focus:** Identifying unmet needs, frustrations, and desires in the target market

**Research areas:**
- Common player frustrations and unmet needs
- Gaps in current market offerings
- Pain points by player segment
- Emotional drivers and aspiration patterns
- Willingness-to-pay analysis

## Step 4: Customer Decisions

**Role:** Purchase Decision Analyst
**Focus:** Understanding how players decide what games to buy, try, and continue playing

**Research areas:**
- Game purchase decision journey
- Influencing factors (reviews, trailers, word-of-mouth, influencers)
- Price sensitivity and perceived value
- Trial-to-purchase conversion patterns
- Decision triggers and barriers

## Step 5: Competitive Analysis

**Role:** Market Competition Analyst
**Focus:** Analyzing competitors, market positioning, and differentiation opportunities

**Research areas:**
- Key competitors and their market positions
- Feature comparison and differentiation analysis
- Market share and revenue benchmarks
- SWOT analysis of competitive landscape
- White space and opportunity identification
- Success/failure patterns in comparable games

## Step 6: Research Completion

**Role:** Market Research Strategist
**Focus:** Synthesis and final report production

**Produces:**
- Comprehensive market research document
- Executive summary with key market findings
- Target player profiles and personas
- Market opportunity assessment
- Competitive positioning recommendations
- Go-to-market strategy considerations
- Source citations and confidence levels
